Who we are

AI Order Processing Feedback ("the tool") is a Google Sheets script used by a small private team to log feedback on an order processing app. You can contact its developer at nadav.automations@gmail.com.

What Google data the tool uses, and why

PermissionWhat the tool does with it
View and manage the spreadsheet the tool is installed inReads and writes feedback entries, descriptions, history and team details in the feedback spreadsheet only. It cannot open any other spreadsheet.
See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this appCreates a Screenshots folder and a Help images folder, saves screenshots and help images in them, shows them in the tool's windows, and removes them when an entry or screenshot is deleted. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
Connect to an external serviceUsed only to download screenshot and help images from Google Drive (Google's own Drive API), so they can be shown in the tool's windows and on the Help tab. No other service is contacted.

Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applicationsShows the tool's own pop-up windows (new entry, edit, screenshots, email preview, team management) inside the spreadsheet.
See your primary Google Account email addressWorks out who is using the tool, so it can match them to their name on the team list, lock rows to their owner and record who added a screenshot.

Where the data is kept

Everything stays in Google: in the feedback spreadsheet and in the Drive folders the tool creates, both owned by the sheet's owner and shared only with team members. The tool has no server or database of its own and keeps no copy of your data anywhere else.

Who can see it

Only the members of the feedback team the sheet's owner has added. The tool does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone else, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

  • Entries and screenshots stay in the spreadsheet and its Drive folder until they are deleted. Deleting an entry or a screenshot through the tool also moves the screenshot file to the Google Drive bin.
  • When someone is removed from the team, their access to the Screenshots folder is removed too.
  • You can withdraw the tool's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To ask for your entries or screenshots to be deleted, email nadav.automations@gmail.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AI Order Processing Feedback's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
