Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Who we are

Nadav Automations makes small tools for private teams, built on Google Workspace. This policy covers this website and every tool listed on it. Contact: nadav.automations@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your data stays in Google (Sheets and Drive) owned by the person who runs the tool. There's no other server or database.
  • Tools are only for people who've been invited. Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service – see GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself sets no cookies.

Some pages remember a few things in your own browser only (its "local storage"), so you don't have to sign in each time:

  • Feedback app: your personal link, and a copy of the entry list so it opens quickly. Click Forget me in the app to remove them.
  • Admin page (owner only): that you're signed in (for up to 30 days), and that this browser is trusted. Click Sign out to end the sign-in.

Feedback app

A tool a small team uses to log feedback on an order processing app. Entries, descriptions and team details are kept in the owner's Google spreadsheet; screenshots in a Google Drive folder the tool creates. Team members can view the spreadsheet and use the app with their personal link.

Only the spreadsheet's owner grants these – team members are never asked.

Activity log

To see how the tools are used, they note when someone opens a tool or adds, changes or views something: their name, what they did, the time, and the kind of device and browser (for example "Computer (Chrome)"). Failed sign-ins to the Admin page are noted too. These notes are kept in a separate Google spreadsheet that only the owner can see, on the owner-only Admin page. The latest 20,000 lines are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.

Who can see your data

Only the owner of each tool and the team members they've invited. The activity log and Admin page are for the owner only.

How long it's kept, and how to remove it

  • Entries and screenshots stay until they're deleted. Deleting an entry also moves its screenshots to the Google Drive bin.
  • When someone is removed from a team, their access to the spreadsheet and their personal link are removed.
  • To ask for your data to be deleted, email nadav.automations@gmail.com.
  • If you've ever given a tool access to your Google Account, you can remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
