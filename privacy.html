Who we are

AI Order Processing Feedback ("the tool") is a Google Sheets script used by a small private team to log feedback on an order processing app. You can contact its developer at nadav.automations@gmail.com.

What Google data the tool uses, and why

View and manage the spreadsheet the tool is installed inReads and writes feedback entries and team details in the feedback spreadsheet only. It cannot open any other spreadsheet.
See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this appCreates a Screenshots folder, saves screenshots in it, shows them in the feedback app, and removes them when an entry is deleted. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Where the data is kept

Everything stays in Google: in the feedback spreadsheet and in the Drive folder the tool creates, both owned by the sheet's owner. Team members can view the spreadsheet, and see screenshots through the feedback app. The tool has no server or database of its own and keeps no copy of your data anywhere else.

The feedback app's pages remember your personal link, and a copy of the entry list so they open quickly, in your own browser only. Click Forget me in the app to remove them.

Who can see it

Only the members of the feedback team the sheet's owner has added. The tool does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone else, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

  • Entries and screenshots stay in the spreadsheet and its Drive folder until they are deleted. Deleting an entry through the tool also moves the screenshot file to the Google Drive bin.
  • When someone is removed from the team, their access to the spreadsheet and their personal link are removed too.
  • You can withdraw the tool's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To ask for your entries or screenshots to be deleted, email nadav.automations@gmail.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AI Order Processing Feedback's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
