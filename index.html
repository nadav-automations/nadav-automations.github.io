What it is

AI Order Processing Feedback is a Google Sheets script used by a small private team to log feedback on an order processing app: bugs, AI errors, suggested improvements and feature requests.

It is not a public service. Only people the sheet's owner adds to the team can use it.

What it does

  • Gives each team member a personal link to a simple feedback app for adding entries, editing their own entries and attaching screenshots.
  • Saves everything in the feedback spreadsheet (which the team can view) and screenshots in a Google Drive folder the tool creates.
  • Adds an Actions menu for the sheet's owner to email lists of entries and summaries to the people they choose, including an optional weekly summary.

Why it asks for permissions

Only the sheet's owner ever grants permissions – team members just use their link. The tool can only see the feedback spreadsheet and the Google Drive files it created itself. The privacy policy lists each permission and why it is needed.
