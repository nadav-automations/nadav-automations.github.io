How the tools work, and what happens to your data

  • No sign-up, no passwordsYou open a tool with your own personal link. There are no accounts to create and nothing to remember.
  • Your link is your keyEach link holds a private key that's unique to you, so the tool knows who you are. Please keep it to yourself – if it's ever shared, it can be replaced.
  • No company data on this websiteThis website only holds the pages you see. Entries, screenshots and team details are never stored here.
  • Where your data goesWhen you save something, it goes over a secure (encrypted) connection straight to the tool's private spreadsheet and file storage in the owner's Google account.
  • Who can see itOnly the owner and the team members invited to that tool. Each tool also notes who did what and when, which only the owner can see.
  • Never sold or sharedNothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising or used to train AI. To have your data deleted, just ask.

The tools don't use cookies. This homepage only counts visits if you accept cookies. Full details are in the privacy policy.
