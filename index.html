How it works

Where your data goes – and where it doesn't

  1. Your link is your keyYou open a tool with your own personal link. It holds a private key unique to you, so the tool knows who you are. Keep it to yourself – it can be replaced if it's ever shared.
  2. Sent securelyWhen you save something, it travels over an encrypted connection – straight past this website, which never stores it.
  3. Kept privatelyIt's saved in the tool's private spreadsheet and file storage in the owner's Google account.
  4. Seen by the team onlyOnly the owner and the team members invited to that tool can see it. Each tool also notes who did what and when, which only the owner can see.
No sign-up, no passwordsNo accounts to create and nothing to remember.
No company data on this websiteIt only holds the pages you see. Entries, screenshots and team details are never stored here.
Never sold or sharedNot sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI. To have your data deleted, just ask.

The tools don't use cookies. This homepage only counts visits if you accept cookies. Full details are in the privacy policy.
